QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What is the Hacker House Goa Builder ID? ▼
It is your official double-sided delegate badge for Hacker House Goa 2026. It features your photo, name, and builder title on the front, and the permanent event details and verification QR code on the back.
How do I download my 2-page print PDF? ▼
Once your ID card is generated, click the "DOWNLOAD PDF" button. The server compiles both Front and Back sides into a single print-ready 2-page PDF file at 432x540pt edge-to-edge.
How does sharing on X (Twitter) work? ▼
Clicking "SHARE ON X" creates a share link with OpenGraph metadata and opens X with the hashtag #FrameInGOA. When posted, your front card image unfurls directly in the timeline.
Is my photo processed securely? ▼
Yes. Photo processing runs via Sharp image engine to auto-correct mobile camera EXIF orientation and crop to a 1:1 square before compositing onto the official badge artwork.
Can I edit my details after generating? ▼
Yes! Click "EDIT DETAILS" beneath your preview cards to return to Step 1 and update your name, title, or photo without losing any inputs.
