QUESTIONS

QUESTION 01: Do I need an account? ▼
No. There is no login, no signup, and no email field anywhere in the flow. You can use all features instantly in your browser.
QUESTION 02: Can I put my whole team in one frame? ▼
Yes. The Team frame takes one to three builders, each with their own photo, stack, and builder class in a single graphic.
QUESTION 03: Is my photo uploaded anywhere? ▼
No. Every frame is rendered on your own client device. Sharing from a phone hands the finished image straight to your app choice.
QUESTION 04: Does the ID card give me a seat? ▼
No, and it does not pretend to. There is no seat number on the graphic because a tool anyone can run cannot award official residency.
QUESTION 05: Does it work on a phone? ▼
Yes, and that is the shortest route. Take a selfie, frame it, share it, all without leaving your browser or installing extra software.
